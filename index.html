I am particularly interested in designing intelligent, context-aware systems, developing autonomous AI agents, and building scalable AI architectures that bridge advanced research with practical applications.

My current environment combines applied research, software engineering, and enterprise technology, with a particular interest in leveraging artificial intelligence to transform complex processes, improve decision-making, and create meaningful real-world impact.
